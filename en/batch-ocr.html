Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How many files can I process at once?
There is no hard limit, but more files take longer. Processing time depends on your browser and device performance.
Q. Can I change the processing order?
Files are processed in the order they were selected. Re-select them in your preferred order to change it.
Q. Are files uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens in your browser. Your files are never sent to any server.
