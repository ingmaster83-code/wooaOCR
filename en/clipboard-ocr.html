Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Are there other ways besides Ctrl+V?
Yes — drag and drop and the file select button also work. All three methods perform the same OCR.
Q. Can I paste immediately after taking a screenshot?
Yes. On Windows: Win+Shift+S then Ctrl+V. On Mac: Cmd+Shift+4 then Cmd+V.
Q. Are files uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens in your browser. Your files are never sent to any server.
