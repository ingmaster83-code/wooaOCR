Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What types of receipts are supported?
Regular receipts, credit card receipts, invoices, and any printed document with amounts are supported.
Q. Does it automatically calculate the total?
The tool detects and highlights amounts in the text, but does not sum them automatically.
Q. Are files uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens in your browser. Your files are never sent to any server.
