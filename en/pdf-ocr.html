Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is the difference between a regular PDF and a scanned PDF?
A regular PDF has a text layer that allows copy-paste. A scanned PDF is image-only and requires OCR. If you can't select text, it's a scanned PDF.
Q. Is there a page limit?
No hard limit, but more pages take longer. We recommend under 10 pages for best performance.
Q. Are files saved to a server?
No. All processing happens in your browser. Your files are never sent anywhere.
