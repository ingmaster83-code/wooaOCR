About WooaOCR

Free online OCR — extract text from images and scanned PDFs, right in your browser

🔍 What is WooaOCR?

WooaOCR is a free online OCR (Optical Character Recognition) service that automatically recognizes and extracts text from photos and scanned documents.

No app installation or account registration required — just open it in your browser and start extracting text. All file processing happens entirely within your browser, so images and PDFs are never uploaded to any server. Sensitive documents can be processed safely and privately.

⚡ Available Tools

🖼️ Image OCR 📑 PDF OCR 📋 Clipboard OCR 📂 Batch OCR 💼 Business Card 🧾 Receipt OCR 📊 Table OCR

✨ Key Features

  • Extract text from JPG, PNG, WebP and other image files
  • OCR recognition from scanned PDF files, page by page
  • Multi-language support: Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, and more
  • Download extracted text as a TXT file
  • Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop
  • Unlimited free use — no account, no payment

🔒 Privacy

All OCR processing in WooaOCR happens 100% within your browser (client-side). We use the open-source Tesseract.js OCR engine. Your image or PDF files are never transmitted to any server.

We use Google Analytics for service usage statistics and Google AdSense for advertising. No personally identifiable information is collected beyond that.
