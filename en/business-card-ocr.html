Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What if recognition quality is poor?
Take a flat, well-lit photo of the card. Higher resolution images produce significantly better results.
Q. Can it read non-English business cards?
Yes, Korean + English mixed recognition is supported, making it ideal for bilingual business cards.
Q. Can the parsed fields be inaccurate?
Auto-parsing may vary by card layout. Check the raw OCR text if a field appears missing or incorrect.
