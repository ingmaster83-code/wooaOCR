Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What kinds of tables are supported?
Grid-style tables, spreadsheet screenshots, and document tables are all supported.
Q. Can the table structure be inaccurate?
Yes — accuracy depends on image quality and table complexity. You can edit the downloaded CSV in Excel.
Q. Does the CSV work in Excel?
Yes. A UTF-8 BOM is included so Excel opens it correctly without encoding problems.
